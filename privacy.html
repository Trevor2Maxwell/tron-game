Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TRON Light Cycles by Nerd Metrics. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our Zoom application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Zoom Profile Information: We collect your Zoom User ID, Name, and Email Address during the authentication process to generate your in-game identity and manage lifetime access subscriptions.
  • Session Data: We track active game sessions natively linked to your given Zoom Meeting Context to connect you automatically with others in your call.
  • Payment Data: All payments are processed securely by Stripe. Nerd Metrics never directly accesses or stores your credit card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide functionality for the TRON Light Cycles game experience inside Zoom, verify paid access, and generate multiplayer networking rooms.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep account and purchase records to manage access to the game. Removing the app from Zoom revokes the app's authorization. Contact us if you want to request deletion of information retained by Nerd Metrics.

5. Contact Us

Email trevor@nerdmetrics.ca with privacy questions or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. We may ask for information needed to verify your request.
